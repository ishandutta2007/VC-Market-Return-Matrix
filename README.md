# VC-Market-Return-Matrix

## Multi-Market Venture Capital Fund Returns Matrix

A comprehensive overview of publicly verifiable fund performance data (Net Internal Rate of Return [IRR] and Total Value to Paid-In [TVPI]) for top multi-market venture capital firms. 

Data is aggregated from institutional Limited Partner (LP) disclosures, including [CalPERS](https://www.calpers.ca.gov) and the [University of California Regents (UCOP)](https://www.ucop.edu).

## Key Insights & Structural Auditing Notes

1. **Vintage Integrity Over Regional Generalization:** Venture fund performance cannot be evaluated without controlling for fund vintage years.
   * Comparing a 2011/2015 fund (e.g., Index Growth II/III at 4.0x–6.0x+ TVPI) to a 2020 fund (e.g., Sequoia US Growth IX at 1.11x TVPI) gives a false picture of regional outperformance. 2020–2022 vintages globally are in early J-curves and were impacted by the post-bubble tech valuation reset, whereas 2015–2018 vintages captured major liquidity events (CrowdStrike, Freshworks, UiPath, Swiggy, King, Adyen).
2. **Fund Structuring Realities:**
   * **Index Ventures:** Does **not** split funds by geography. "Growth II/III" and "Growth IV/V" are consecutive vintages of the exact same transatlantic fund series investing equally in North America and Europe.
   * **Sequoia Capital & Lightspeed:** Use global fund deployment for Europe and Israel rather than raising dedicated regional standalone entities. Sequoia operated dedicated Israel vehicles (*Sequoia Capital Israel I–V*) until 2016 before folding the strategy into global vehicles.
   * **Accel:** Operates genuinely autonomous, regional partnership franchises with isolated fund vehicles (**Accel US**, **Accel London / Europe**, and **Accel India**).
3. **Geopolitical Separation of Cross-Border Franchises:**
   * **Sequoia Capital:** Formally completed its tripartite split in March 2024 into **Sequoia Capital** (US & Europe), **HongShan** (China, led by Neil Shen), and **Peak XV Partners** (India & Southeast Asia, led by Shailendra Singh).
   * **GGV Capital:** Formally dissolved its cross-border structure on March 29, 2024, splitting into **Notable Capital** (US & European investments) and **Granite Asia** (APAC, Southeast Asia, India, and China, based in Singapore).
   * **Lightspeed China Partners:** Rebranded to **Luminous Ventures** in late 2025, operating as an independent entity separate from US-based Lightspeed Venture Partners.
4. **Verified Institutional Sources:**
   * **UCOP (UC Regents):** *Alternative Investments Private Equity Disclosures* (reporting Net IRR and TVPI multiples for HongShan China Growth V [12.2% IRR, 1.95x TVPI], Peak XV / India VI [21.0% IRR, 2.86x TVPI], and Sequoia US Growth IX [3.2% IRR, 1.11x TVPI]).
   * **CalPERS:** *Private Equity Program Performance Reviews & AIV Disclosures* (tracking Lightspeed Venture Partners Select and Inception/Ignite vehicles).
   * **LACERA / LACERS:** *Private Equity Portfolio Performance Reports* (audited metrics for GGV Capital VI, VI Plus, and IV).
   * **State of Michigan Retirement System (SMRS):** *Investment Advisory Committee Quarterly Reports* (tracking Accel XIII, Accel Growth, and Accel London).
